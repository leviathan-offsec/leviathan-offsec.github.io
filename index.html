03 / Method & Doctrine

Engineering Philosophy

Mass internet-wide active scanning is noisy, expensive, and fragile. The Leviathan methodology focuses on zero-packet passive correlation, deep binary and firmware reverse engineering, and explainable risk modeling.

  • ✓ Low Infrastructure Overhead: All tooling is designed to run efficiently on low-power ARM64 nodes (Raspberry Pi 5) or standard developer machines.
  • ✓ Supply-Chain Focus: Auditing foundational packages where a single flaw ripples across tens of millions of downstream installs.
  • ✓ Explainable Risk: Zero black-box metrics. Every risk score is backed by deterministic formulas against verified threat feeds (CISA KEV, FIRST EPSS).
