What changed this week.

One email a week. Perimeter changes that matter and CVEs that are actually exploitable in what you're running — triaged, ranked, and cut down to the part you need to act on.

  • Change-only alerts. No repeat noise, no "new CVE published" filler.
  • Ranked by real risk — CVSS and EPSS and CISA KEV, not a single opaque score.
  • Free. The paid tier is realtime; this is the weekly read.
  • No spam, ever. Unsubscribe in one click, and we don't sell your address.

By subscribing you agree to receive the weekly digest. That's it. Unsubscribe any time.

Responsible disclosure. We publish analysis and reproduction steps for patched issues. We do not ship working exploits for unpatched vulnerabilities. If you've found something in your own stack, email us directly instead of posting it here.
